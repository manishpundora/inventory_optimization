--- THE SAME TEST AT AGGREGATE PLANNING LEVEL ---
Nothing above says forecasting is worthless. It says forecasting a single
intermittent SKU is hard. Buyers do not only plan SKU by SKU - they commit
container space, warehouse labour and working capital against the total.
So the same methods are re-run on total weekly units, where the pooled
series is far smoother.
